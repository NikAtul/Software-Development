Animation as a Creative Industry: State of The Art

24-25 November 2021

This study discusses the role of the creative industries in global economic development, with a particular focus on animation as one of the main sub-sectors. Animation is seen to grow along with the advancement of computer technology and plays an important role in the production chain and process of the creative industry. In addition, this study also examines animation business models in several developing countries and the Southeast Asian region. The results of the study show that the animation industry offers great opportunities, whether through outsourcing services or the development of original intellectual property (IP), making it an increasingly important field in the global creative economy.

Virtual Reality in Education

This study explains that Virtual Reality (VR) allows users to interact with a three-dimensional computer-generated environment, whether realistic, scientific, or imaginative. VR technology is increasingly being used in various educational fields such as science, archaeology, history, and architecture because it provides a more immersive learning experience. The main advantage of VR is its interactive and experiential nature, which helps students understand concepts that are difficult to explain through traditional methods. In addition, this study also emphasizes how multisensory VR systems can enhance the learning process from a cognitive and sensory psychology perspective, making it a very effective educational tool.

Current and Future Photography Techniques in Aesthetic Surgery

29 November 2021

This article discusses the importance of using modern imaging technology in the field of aesthetic medicine. Among the important factors for obtaining high-quality images are the use of high-resolution cameras, stable tripods, appropriate studio lighting, standard backgrounds, and consistent patient positioning. 3D and 4D photography technologies offer advantages in helping patients understand the expected results of treatment as well as improving service quality and safety. In addition, artificial intelligence (AI) has the potential to assist in image processing and the production of 3D models after procedures. However, the use of smartphones should be done with caution as it can change the patient's perception of their appearance, while cloud-based storage provides convenience but still carries the risk of data leakage.
